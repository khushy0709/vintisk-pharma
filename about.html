CORPORATE PROFILE

Building a Trusted Pharmaceutical Brand

Vintisk Pharmaceuticals is a quality-focused pharmaceutical marketing company dedicated to delivering accessible, affordable, and innovative branded generic medicines across the domestic Indian market.

WHO WE ARE

Dedication Towards Saving Lives

Founded in 2021, Vintisk Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. operates as a pharmaceutical marketing company building a strong, doctor-trusted presence in the domestic Indian healthcare ecosystem.

We market our own branded generic pharmaceutical portfolio across essential therapeutic segments. Our products are manufactured through contracts and long-term partnerships with established pharmaceutical manufacturers operating WHO-GMP and ISO-certified manufacturing facilities.

OUR VISION

Build a trusted pharmaceutical brand focused on quality, affordability, innovation and wider access to healthcare.

We envision long-term healthcare partnerships that make essential, reliable therapeutic medicines consistently available to communities across India.

OUR MISSION

To develop and market quality-focused branded generic pharmaceutical solutions while building trusted partnerships across the healthcare ecosystem.

Our commitment is anchored on ethics, clinical transparency, and partnering with top-tier certified manufacturers to supply safe, reliable formulations.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES

The Values Guiding Vintisk

Our core values define our corporate ethics, our healthcare relationships, and our commitment to patient well-being.

01

Quality

Ensuring all marketed products are sourced from WHO-GMP and ISO-certified manufacturing facilities adhering to pharmacopoeial specifications.

02

Integrity

Conducting business with absolute transparency, statutory compliance, and ethical detailing with medical practitioners.

03

Reliability

Maintaining consistent supply chains and responsive stock availability for distributors, hospitals, and pharmacies.

04

Innovation

Continuously optimizing drug delivery formats, packaging stability, and therapeutic combinations to serve evolving patient needs.

05

Partnership

Fostering collaborative, win-win relationships with medical representatives, stockists, retailers, and certified manufacturing partners.
