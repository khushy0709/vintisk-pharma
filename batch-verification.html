PACKAGING ASSISTANCE

Where to Find Your Batch Number

Every genuine Vintisk Pharma marketed product features an official batch identification code for complete traceability.

1

Outer Packaging Flap

Inspect the side or top flap of the product carton box. Look for embossed or inkjet printed lines starting with B.No. or Batch.

2

Blister / Foil Strip

For tablets and capsules, the batch number is crimped or printed along the sealed edge or reverse side of the aluminium/PVC blister strip.

3

Bottle / Syrup Label

For liquid oral suspensions and syrups, the batch code is printed directly on the bottle label alongside the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) and Expiry Date.
