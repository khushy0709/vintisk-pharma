GOVERNANCE & STANDARDS

Quality Through Trusted Manufacturing Partnerships

Vintisk Pharmaceuticals is committed to offering quality-focused pharmaceutical products through established manufacturing partners operating WHO-GMP and ISO-certified manufacturing facilities.

OUR COMMITMENT

Quality Commitment

As a pharmaceutical marketing company, Vintisk recognizes that patient trust depends on strict therapeutic efficacy and chemical purity. We enforce rigorous quality criteria before any formulation is selected, branded, or distributed.

We do not compromise on sourcing: every single branded generic product in our basket is produced exclusively through established contract manufacturers with demonstrated compliance records.

The Partner Model

Vintisk manages brand development and clinical marketing, while contracted manufacturing partners execute batch production within audited WHO-GMP and ISO-certified facilities.

MANUFACTURING AUDIT

WHO-GMP & ISO-Certified Partner Facilities

Our partner facilities feature modern cleanroom engineering, qualified Purified Water (PW/WFI) generation, and multi-stage testing laboratories equipped for precision pharmacopoeial testing (IP / BP / USP).

WHO-GMP Alignment

Contracted partner facilities hold verified WHO-GMP compliance certificates.

ISO-Certified Systems

Operating under ISO accredited quality management and environmental controls.

Batch Assay Testing

HPLC testing verifying active molecule potency and dissolution before release.

Stability Chambers

Zone IV climatic stability verification across tropical temperature ranges.

END-TO-END INTEGRITY

Development, Packaging & Regulatory Standards

Ensuring every physical pack reaching pharmacies, clinics, and hospitals meets statutory drug laws and protective requirements.

Quality-Focused Product Development

Careful selection of API quality, excipient profiles, and pharmacokinetic compatibility to ensure bioequivalent drug release and therapeutic reliability.

  • ✔ Pharmacopoeial monograph compliance (IP / BP / USP)
  • ✔ Optimal bioavailability and therapeutic index
  • ✔ Rigorous excipient chemical stability

Packaging & Supply Integrity

Moisture-barrier Alu-Alu blisters, blister foils, amber bottles, and sealed ampoules engineered to resist humidity, heat, and transit impact across India.

  • ✔ High-barrier tropical Alu-Alu packaging
  • ✔ Tamper-evident seals on cartons and bottles
  • ✔ Clear batch numbering, manufacturing and expiry dates

Regulatory & Ethical Standards

Adherence to Indian Drugs and Cosmetics Act regulations, ethical marketing codes, batch release COA verification, and proactive pharmacovigilance tracking.

  • ✔ Batch Certificate of Analysis (COA) verification
  • ✔ Compliance with Drugs and Cosmetics Rules
  • ✔ Ethical detailing and transparency with doctors
